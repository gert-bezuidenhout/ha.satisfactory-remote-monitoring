## Dashboard cards

The integration now bundles three cards. Restart Home Assistant after updating, refresh your browser, and use **Add card** on a dashboard.

- **Satisfactory Player** shows the selected player's Pioneer, segmented health bar, and online status.
- **Satisfactory Coupons** shows the printable coupon count.
- **Satisfactory Circuit Power** shows maximum consumption and capacity by circuit group.

The integration serves the card module and Pioneer image. No manual resource registration is required.
